## Spotify Streaming Performance & Listening Analysis

## About the Project

Music is a part of everyday life, and everyone has different listening habits. Some people listen to music for hours, while others prefer particular artists, tracks, or times of the day. In this project, I worked with Spotify listening history data to explore listening patterns and understand how music is played over time.
The main focus of this project was to analyze streaming activity, listening duration, popular artists and tracks, skipped songs, and listening habits across different days and time periods. I used data analysis and visualization to turn listening history into meaningful insights.

## Project Objectives

The objective of this project was to understand Spotify listening behavior by measuring total streams, minutes and hours listened, average listening time, unique artists, unique tracks, and skip rate. I also explored monthly listening trends, popular artists and tracks, completion status, and listening patterns across weekdays and different times of the day.

## Dataset Description

The project uses a dataset named spotify_history.csv. It contains listening history information, including track names, artist names, timestamps, listening duration in milliseconds, and whether a track was skipped. These fields help analyze streaming activity, listening time, and music preferences.

## Tools and Technologies Used

I used Power BI to prepare the data, create calculated measures, and build visualizations. DAX was used to calculate important metrics, while Power Query was used for data preparation and checking data types. The dashboard visuals helped present the listening patterns in a clear and organized way.

## Work Performed

I started by importing the Spotify listening history dataset into Power BI, checking missing values, and verifying data types. Then, I created calculated measures to analyze total streams, total minutes and hours listened, average listening time, unique artists, unique tracks, skipped tracks, and skip rate.
I also created additional columns for year, month, day, hour, weekday, listening category, time period, completion status, listening hours, and track length category.
To explore the data, I created different visualizations, including KPI cards, line charts, bar charts, donut charts, pie charts, column charts, area charts, stacked bar charts, scatter plots, ribbon charts, matrix tables, funnel charts, and a gauge chart. These visuals were designed to compare listening trends, identify popular artists and tracks, and understand when and how music was played.

## Key Learnings

Through this project, I gained practical experience in Power BI, data preparation, DAX measures, calculated columns, and dashboard design. I learned how to calculate important KPIs, analyze listening trends over time, compare artists and tracks, and represent data using different visualizations.

## Conclusion

This project helped me understand how listening history data can be used to explore music habits and streaming performance. By analyzing listening duration, popular artists, tracks, skipped songs, and time-based trends, I developed a better understanding of how data can reveal patterns in everyday music listening. It also strengthened my practical skills in Power BI and data analytics.
